      How certification works

      Fair, transparent and built to help you pass.

      Your final result

      50% Module assessments 50% Final exam

      Your best attempt at each module counts. After the final, you see how you did in each module as a band:

      • Strong
      • OK
      • Needs work

      • Unlimited attempts

        Try again as often as you need. Only your best attempt counts.

      • Hints, not answers

        After each module assessment you see your score, which questions to revisit, and a hint for each.

      • A clear route to the final

        The final unlocks once you’ve done the pre-test and answered every question in every counted module part.

      • Scored securely

        All marking happens on the server, so results are fair and consistent for everyone.

      Questions

      Good to know before you start

      Help centre · Email the support team

      Who can join?

      Anyone aged 16 or over. If you’re 16 or 17, we’ll ask a parent or guardian to confirm consent by email before your account starts.

      How do I register?

      Sign up with your own email address and choose a batch that’s open for registration. Batches open when a mentor or the programme team opens them.

      Can I skip ahead or go back to a topic?

      Yes. Learning content is never locked, so you can open any module in any order.

      What happens if I don’t pass an assessment?

      Nothing bad. Review the hints and try again. Attempts are unlimited and only your best attempt counts. The pass mark is 50%.

      How do I get my certificate?

      Download it from your dashboard once it’s issued. Certificates are in English and include a QR code for public verification.

      Ready to become a Cyber First Responder?

      Pick an open batch, preview the name on your certificate, and start your first module today. It’s free.
