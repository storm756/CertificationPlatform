Privacy notice

How the programme collects, uses and protects your information.

Content slot: the approved privacy notice (version-numbered) from CyberPeace legal goes here. Learners confirm the current version when they register.

What the platform stores

  • Your certificate name, email, date of birth, preferred language and batch
  • Units you complete, your answers and scores, and certificate records
  • For learners aged 16–17: the guardian’s name, email and consent record

Things worth knowing

  • No behavioural or time-on-task tracking for learners under 18.
  • Films play from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube may still set its own cookies once a film is played.
  • Certificates are downloaded from the site, not emailed. The public verification page shows only name, qualification, grade, issue date and status.
