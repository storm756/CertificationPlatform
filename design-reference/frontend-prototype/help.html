Help

How can we help?

Answers to common questions about joining, learning, assessments and certificates.

Who can join?

Anyone aged 16 or over. If you are 16 or 17, a parent or guardian confirms by email before your account starts. Under-16s can’t register.

How do I register?

Sign up with your own email and pick a batch that is accepting registrations. Batches are opened by a mentor or the programme team, sometimes for a set window. If none are open, ask your trainer.

Why is my certificate name locked?

You type it exactly as it should be printed, and we show a preview. After that only your mentor or an admin can change it, and the change is logged.

Is any learning locked?

No. Every film, reading, handout and practice check is open from day one. Go in any order and come back any time.

How are modules marked?

Each module is worth 100 marks: Part A (multiple choice, 50) and Part B (interactive, 50). Your best attempt at each part counts, and the pass mark is 50%.

What do I see after an assessment?

Your score, which questions were correct or incorrect, and a hint for each. Never the correct option, so the assessment stays fair for everyone.

What is a safety gate?

Some choices could put a person at risk, such as paying a ‘recovery agent’ or asking a victim to send an intimate image. If you make one, the attempt shows its score but doesn’t count. Read the hint and try again.

When does the final unlock?

When you have taken the pre-test and submitted every counted part of every module with all questions answered. You don’t need to have passed them yet.

How is my result worked out?

Overall = 50% of the average of your nine module scores + 50% of your best final. Grades: A+ from 90, A from 80, B from 60, C from 50.

How do I get my certificate?

When you’re eligible, it appears on your dashboard after your mentor approves it, or immediately if your batch has auto-issue on. Download it from the site. It is never emailed. Certificates are in English.

Can I switch language?

Yes, any time, even during an assessment. Your answers are kept. Some translations are still drafts and are labelled.

I forgot my password

Use ‘Forgot your password?’ on the sign-in page. If you can’t reach your email, your mentor can give you a one-time reset link that works for one hour.

Still stuck?

Talk to your mentor first, since they can see your batch. Or email the support team.

Need help right now?

Money lost to fraud: call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in. Anyone in danger: call 112.
