Download a module's sheet, fill the Hindi, Marathi or Gujarati column, and upload it back. Every new or changed line is saved as a draft. A reviewer for that language, who did not upload the line, marks it reviewed in Translations. Lessons show drafts with a "draft translation" tag; assessments show reviewed lines only (D24).

The upload is all-or-nothing. It refuses unknown keys, changed [[!markers]] or {placeholders}, < or > that the English does not have, and a blank cell where a translation already exists.
